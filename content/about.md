Trending Niches publishes plain-English guides to topics that people are only starting to search for: new kinds of products, hobbies, home technology, health and lifestyle trends and services that are growing steadily but are not yet mainstream.

## How topics are chosen
Every day we look at public signals of interest in the UK and the US, including Google's trending searches and long-run Wikipedia reading figures. A topic only gets a guide if interest has been rising week after week for months, rather than spiking for a day because it was in the news.

## How guides are written
Each guide is researched from sources that are named at the bottom of the page, with official bodies and primary sources preferred. We do not invent figures, prices or reviews, and we include downsides and uncertainties. Guides are written with AI assistance and checked against their sources before publishing.

## Not advice
Guides are general information. For health, money or legal decisions, speak to a qualified professional such as your GP, a pharmacist or a regulated adviser.

## Corrections
If you spot something wrong or out of date, the sources listed on each page are the best place to check the current position, and we update guides as the facts change.
