## Where to buy in the US

### Official site
stickerbox.com sells the device, bundles and all paper types. Orders ship from California after one to two business days of handling. The delivery page lists next-day (1 business day), express (3-4) and standard (5-7) options. Unopened boxes can be returned within 30 days of delivery; opened boxes cannot be refunded but defective ones are replaced. There is a six-month limited warranty.

### Amazon
Stickerbox is sold on Amazon in the US. Tom's Guide reported a Prime Day deal of $103 (from $129) that ran to 26 June 2026, and said discounts had been rare. We could not open the Amazon listing to confirm today's price. ## Can you buy Stickerbox in the UK?

Not officially, as of October 2026. Hapiko's delivery page says it ships within the United States only, the box comes with a US-standard power adapter, and the launch press release described the product as US only. We found no listing on a UK retailer's site from Hapiko.

A European retailer, Heyup, has a Stickerbox product page, but when we checked it showed no price and only a sign-up to be told when it launches. Treat any UK release as unconfirmed until Hapiko announces it.

### Watch out for lookalikes
UK deal sites carry cheaper "AI voice printers" that look similar. One Wowcher deal in October 2026 was £29.99 plus £3.99 postage, sold by a different company, with a page heading that used the word "Stickerbox" even though the product description did not. That is a different product from a different maker, with its own paper, app and data practices. It may be fine, but you cannot assume Hapiko's safety filters or privacy promises apply to it.

### If you import one
Some UK families use a US parcel-forwarding address. If you do, bear in mind:

- **Import tax.** GOV.UK says VAT is due on most goods from outside the UK. For goods over £135, VAT is collected before delivery, and customs duty may also apply; VAT is worked out on the goods plus postage, packaging, insurance and any duty. The courier sends a bill and usually holds the parcel for about three weeks if it is not paid. A $129.99 Starter Set (about £99) plus forwarding costs can tip over £135.
- **Plug.** The adapter is a US-standard plug, so you will need a suitable UK adapter or power supply. Check the label on the adapter before using it on UK mains.
- **Paper.** Refills also ship to the US only, so every refill means another forwarded parcel. Buying several packs at once makes more sense.
- **Warranty and returns.** Returns go back to a US address at your cost, and Hapiko's terms are written for US customers.
- **Data law.** Hapiko's privacy policy refers to US law and does not mention UK GDPR. See [Is Stickerbox safe for kids?](/stickerbox/is-stickerbox-safe-for-kids/).

## What it costs to run

The Everymom's reviewer said her child made 1,273 stickers in nine months. On Hapiko's figure of 66 stickers a roll, that is about 20 rolls, or roughly $40 of standard paper on top of the device. Using only holographic or transparent paper would cost about two and a half times as much.
