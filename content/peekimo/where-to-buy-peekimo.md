## How to spot a fake Peekimo

We found no reports of counterfeit Peekimos so far. Popular toys can attract lookalikes, though, so it is worth knowing what the real one looks like.

### What a genuine Peekimo has
- **Brand.** Made by Spin Master. European listings name Spin Master Intl BV as the manufacturer.
- **Box contents.** One Peekimo, one lanyard, one carabiner ring, a quick start guide and an instruction sheet (per Smyths).
- **Battery.** One AAA battery, included and already fitted (per Target and Smyths).
- **Age marking.** 5+ on US and UK retail listings, plus a small-parts choking warning for under-3s.
- **Barcode.** Very lists EAN 681147152229 for the pink Penny. You can compare this with the barcode on the box.
- **Features.** A pod with a touch bar, button and mood light, more than 100 reactions, and five unlockable games.

### Warning signs
- A price well below £23.99 or $24.97 from an unknown seller.
- No Spin Master name or logo on the box, or a slightly different spelling of "Peekimo".
- Stock photos only, or photos that do not match the retailer images.
- Sellers who will not say where they are shipping from.
- A missing lanyard and carabiner, or a different battery type.

### Safer ways to buy
- Buy from Smyths, Very, Target, Walmart (sold by Walmart) or Amazon (sold by Amazon) rather than marketplace sellers.
- On eBay or other resale apps, ask for photos of the sealed box and barcode, and pay with a method that offers buyer protection.
- Electronic toys from unknown sellers may not meet UK or US safety rules, so avoid unbranded lookalikes for young children.
