In the UK, branded Crazy Fun dumpling squishies are sold in supermarkets and toy shops, including Morrisons, Tesco, Asda, B&M, Smyths, The Entertainer and Argos, for about £4-£6 each. Genuine NeeDoh in the UK comes through its official distributor, Bigjigs Toys. In the US, Five Below is the main home of the RMS Mystery Dumpling at about $5, and NeeDoh is sold at Target, Walmart, CVS, Walgreens and Barnes & Noble, usually for under $15. Resale prices on Vinted, eBay and marketplaces can be several times higher, and that is also where most fakes turn up.

*Prices checked October 2026.* They come from the retailer pages listed in the sources, or from news reports where we say so. Stock changes daily and several NeeDoh lines were sold out when we checked.

## How to spot a fake or unsafe knock-off

Not every unbranded squishy is dangerous, and not every lookalike is a counterfeit. The problem is copies that pretend to be a brand, or that skip testing. Use these checks together:

### Packaging
- **NeeDoh:** Bigjigs says genuine NeeDoh has the NeeDoh logo on the base seal and/or Schylling branding underneath, and comes in branded packaging. A Texas toy shop told KBTX that real NeeDoh boxes are matte with safety tape and the toy is not sealed in a plastic bag inside.
- **Crazy Fun dumplings:** GlitterBeam says genuine ones carry RMS and Crazy Fun branding.
- **Fake tie-ins:** South Carolina officials seized fake NeeDoh "Nice Cubes" with Sprite, Pepsi and McDonald's branding; Schylling does not partner with those companies. Westminster Council found fakes with Starbucks and Coca-Cola logos.
- **Missing details:** the CPSC says to watch for misspellings, poor printing and no maker, importer, age grading or warnings. In the UK, Rhondda Cynon Taf Council's checklist is: maker name and address, importer if made outside the UK, UKCA or CE mark, batch code and clear warnings.

### Smell and feel
- Bigjigs says real NeeDoh has little or no smell and feels firm. Fakes may smell of chemicals or feel sticky, greasy, too soft or full of air bubbles.
- Several UK councils say to bin any squishy with a strong chemical or petrol-like smell. One distributor told the Jersey Evening Post that smells can build up in sealed packs, but a strong smell is still the main warning sign.

### Price and seller
- The CPSC says to be wary of prices far below established shops.
- MalwareTips reported fake NeeDoh websites that copy branding, claim to have every sold-out item in stock, offer large bundle discounts, and then send nothing or a counterfeit. A domain containing "NeeDoh" or "Schylling" does not make it official.
- Which? warns that fakes often circulate second-hand. On Vinted or eBay, ask for photos of the base, the packaging and the label.
- Pay by credit card or another method with buyer protection.
