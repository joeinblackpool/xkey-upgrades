Where to buy Clickeez in the UK and US

Clickeez prices at Smyths, The Entertainer, Argos, Target, Walmart and Five Below, checked October 2026, plus tips on blind packs and marketplace sellers.

In the UK, the widest Clickeez range is at Smyths Toys, where packs run from £4.99 for a two-pack to £29.99 for the Keyboard Mega Pack. The Entertainer and Argos also stock them. In the US, Clickeez launched at Five Below and are sold at Target, Walmart and Amazon, with launch prices from $4.99 to $29.99. If a listing is well above those prices, it is almost always a marketplace reseller rather than the retailer itself.

Prices checked 16 October 2026 on the pages listed in the sources, unless another date is given. Stock changes quickly before Christmas.

Tips before you buy

  • Check the seller, not just the shop. On Amazon and Walmart, marketplace sellers can list at any price. The retailer's own listings are usually close to launch price.
  • Know what is hidden. Two-packs show one key and hide one; five-packs show three and hide two. You cannot choose the hidden ones.
  • Use the collector's guide inside each pack to see which characters are left in a series before buying more.
  • Think about the keyboard first if your child is keen. The Mega Pack works out cheaper per key than two-packs (12 keys for £29.99 or $29.99 versus 2 for £4.99 or $4.99), and it gives the collection somewhere to live.
  • Avoid unbranded lookalikes. Goliath says copycats have appeared. See how to spot them.
  • Small parts. Target's and Smyths' listings carry a choking-hazard warning: not for children under 3.
